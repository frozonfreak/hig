Common questions

Where do I start?

HIG-QUICK.md is the whole onboarding path — about five minutes. Pin that file, then tell agents: Follow The Web HIG Quick Reference. Step-by-step: Adopt.

Is this a component library?

No. You keep your design system and stack; the HIG defines behavior, states, accessibility, performance, and security UX.
